# FAQ Structure Improvement – Case Study

Improving FAQ usability through structured design, inspired by TechScribe principles.

---

## Overview
This project demonstrates how restructuring FAQs can improve clarity, readability, and usability.  
The approach is applied to a sample **Gmail troubleshooting guide**.

---

## Problem
Traditional FAQs often present a flat list of questions without organization.  
This makes it harder for users to quickly find relevant information.

---

## Approach
Instead of listing questions randomly, this case study applies a structured method:

- Group questions by **user intent**
- Add clear, descriptive **category labels**
- Improve **scanability** and navigation

---

## Example

### Before (Unstructured FAQ)
- Why can’t I sign in to my Gmail account?  
- What should I do if I forgot my Gmail password?  
- Why can’t I send attachments in Gmail?  

### After (Structured FAQ)

**Account Access**
- Why can’t I sign in to my Gmail account?  

**Password Recovery**
- What should I do if I forgot my Gmail password?  

**Sending Emails**
- Why can’t I send attachments in Gmail?  

---

## Outcome
- Easier to scan  
- Faster problem identification  
- Better alignment with user needs
